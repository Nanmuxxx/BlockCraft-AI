## Recommendation and proposed limits — NOT APPROVAL / NOT CONFIGURATION

For an initial reproducible text-to-image integration candidate, prefer **Qwen `qwen-image-2.0-2026-03-03`, Singapore, n=1, explicit 1024×1024**, conditional on approved regional eligibility and official schema/account re-verification. Reason: pinned model, documented PNG contract, simple per-image price. Its SSRF-safe second download adds implementation work. Evaluate 2.1 Turbo as the cheaper alternative once its exact API contract/version is verified; there is no measured quality evidence to justify replacing the pinned candidate now. OpenAI direct base64 is an alternative if its region and demonstrable worst-case price envelope are approved; token estimates alone must not authorize dispatch. No existing DeepSeek allowance transfers to any choice.

Suggested **new** isolated approval: max **3 attempts**, **$0.25 total**, **$0.05 reservation/attempt** for the $0.035 pinned Qwen option. Three nominal successful images ~$0.105; conservative reservations $0.15. These numbers are proposed independently of historical text budgets. They do not approve a test, create a budget or cap supplier charges from other clients.

Suggested later restricted-trial app admission caps: **$1 global/day**, **$0.25/user/day**, **$0.50/project lifetime**, plus **$5 total approval lifetime**, fixed-price currency USD, concurrency 1, n=1, no automatic retry/failover. Reserve $0.05 atomically in every relevant scope: at most 20/day globally, 5/user/day and 10/project lifetime under conservative reservations. Retaining conservative debits gives fewer requests than a refund-to-actual policy. Changing any limit requires a new approved policy, never a ledger reset. At rollover, unresolved debt remains encumbered and the approval stays halted.

Reserve must cover a documented maximum charge for the **exact** enabled operation and region; $0.05 is not a universal cap for arbitrary models/sizes, taxes or provider behavior. Refresh price evidence at least before each REAL session, expire within 24h (earlier on change), pin model/operation/region/currency and reject unverified prices. App hard admission limits require transactional durability; dashboard alerts and rate limits alone do not provide them. Owner-approved provider-side controls, if available, supplement the app; none was configured here.

## Cost-relevant failures

- Invalid input, anonymous/unauthorized project, stale price or exhausted budget: reject before reservation/egress; no model cost.
- Provider 401/403, moderation block, 429 or 5xx: safe error; no automatic paid retry. Retain reserve until a trustworthy provider outcome proves actual cost; a status code alone need not prove the server did no work.
- Dispatch followed by timeout/disconnect/crash/missing usage: UNCERTAIN, maximum reserve retained and approval halted for reconciliation. Local abort is not a refund.
- Successful billed generation followed by malformed/oversize/unsafe image, failed download or persistence: do not adopt; retain known charge (or full reserve if unknown), never regenerate automatically.
- Duplicate request: same scoped key never dispatches again. Changed payload conflicts. Restart, queue timeout, new browser tab, new worktree and daily rollover must not reset debt.
